🧱 CORE MODULE: Supplier ESG Audit Engine

🔹 1. Supplier Registry (Foundation)

You need a structured supplier database.

Required fields:

Company Details
	•	Legal name
	•	Trading name
	•	Company number
	•	Country
	•	Industry type

Contacts
	•	Primary ESG contact (name, email, phone)
	•	Secondary contact
	•	Finance / operations contact

Logistics
	•	Shipping company(s)
	•	Freight method (road, sea, air)
	•	Regions supplied from

Risk Tags (important)
	•	High / Medium / Low ESG risk
	•	Material type (e.g. chemicals, metals, plastics)
	•	Critical supplier (yes/no)

⸻

🔹 2. ESG Audit Template Builder

You need reusable templates.

Categories to include:

🌱 Environmental
	•	Carbon emissions tracking (Scope 1, 2, 3)
	•	Waste management practices
	•	Water usage
	•	Materials sourcing (sustainable / certified)

⸻

👥 Social
	•	Labour practices
	•	Health & safety policies
	•	Modern slavery compliance
	•	Employee wellbeing

⸻

🏛️ Governance
	•	Code of conduct
	•	Anti-bribery policy
	•	Data privacy compliance
	•	Certifications (ISO, etc.)

⸻

📦 Supply Chain Specific (your edge)
	•	Origin of materials
	•	Traceability of inputs
	•	Sub-supplier visibility
	•	Transport emissions

⸻

🔹 3. Audit Distribution Engine

This is where you get slick.

Features:
	•	Send audit via:
	•	Secure link (preferred)
	•	Email invite
	•	Supplier:
	•	Completes online
	•	Uploads evidence (PDFs, certs)
	•	Track:
	•	Sent date
	•	Opened
	•	Completed
	•	Overdue

⸻

🔹 4. Scoring + Risk Engine (CRITICAL)

Don’t just collect data. Score it.

Output:
	•	ESG Score (0–100)
	•	Risk rating:
	•	Low
	•	Medium
	•	High
	•	Flags:
	•	Missing policies
	•	High-risk materials
	•	Non-compliance

⸻

🔹 5. Audit Trail (this ties to SOC 2)

Every audit must be:
	•	Timestamped
	•	Versioned
	•	Locked after submission

Track:
	•	Who sent it
	•	Who completed it
	•	What changed
	•	When it was approved

👉 This aligns with SOC 2 requirements

⸻

🔹 6. 12-Month Recurrence Engine (your automation win)

This is where you get ahead of everyone.

Build:
	•	Audit frequency:
	•	Default: 12 months
	•	Configurable (6, 12, 24)
	•	System automatically:
	•	Schedules next audit
	•	Sends reminders:
	•	30 days before
	•	7 days before
	•	Overdue alerts
	•	Dashboard:
	•	“Suppliers overdue”
	•	“Audits expiring soon”

⸻

🔹 7. Reporting Layer (board-level output)

You need:
	•	Supplier ESG summary dashboard
	•	Risk heatmap
	•	Compliance % across suppliers
	•	Exportable PDF report
