🧾 ENVIR0IQ SUPPLIER ESG AUDIT

(Structured, Scorable, Audit-Ready)

⸻

🔹 SECTION 0: SUPPLIER DETAILS (Required)
	•	Legal company name
	•	Trading name
	•	Company registration number
	•	Country of operation
	•	Industry / sector
	•	Description of goods/services supplied

Contacts
	•	Primary ESG contact (name, email, phone)
	•	Secondary contact
	•	Senior responsible officer (Director / GM)

⸻

🌱 SECTION 1: ENVIRONMENTAL

Carbon & Emissions
	•	Do you measure your greenhouse gas emissions? (Yes/No)
	•	Which scopes do you measure? (Scope 1 / 2 / 3)
	•	Provide your last 12 months emissions data (upload)
	•	Do you have emissions reduction targets? (Yes/No)
	•	Are targets science-based? (Yes/No)

⸻

Energy
	•	Total annual energy consumption (kWh)
	•	% renewable energy used
	•	Do you have an energy reduction plan? (Yes/No)

⸻

Materials & Sourcing
	•	List key materials supplied
	•	Are materials sustainably sourced? (Yes/No)
	•	Do you hold certifications (e.g. FSC, recycled content)? (Upload)
	•	Country of origin for materials

⸻

Waste Management
	•	Do you track waste output? (Yes/No)
	•	% waste recycled
	•	Hazardous waste handling process (describe)

⸻

Water
	•	Do you track water usage? (Yes/No)
	•	Any water reduction initiatives? (Yes/No)

⸻

👥 SECTION 2: SOCIAL

Labour Practices
	•	Do you comply with local labour laws? (Yes/No)
	•	Do you have a formal employment contract for all staff? (Yes/No)
	•	Average weekly working hours

⸻

Modern Slavery
	•	Do you have a Modern Slavery / Human Rights policy? (Upload)
	•	Do you assess your own suppliers for slavery risk? (Yes/No)

⸻

Health & Safety
	•	Do you have a Health & Safety policy? (Upload)
	•	Recordable incident rate (last 12 months)
	•	Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate (LTIFR)

⸻

Diversity & Inclusion
	•	Do you track workforce diversity? (Yes/No)
	•	% female workforce
	•	% female leadership

⸻

🏛️ SECTION 3: GOVERNANCE

Policies & Ethics
	•	Do you have a Code of Conduct? (Upload)
	•	Anti-bribery / corruption policy? (Upload)
	•	Whistleblower policy? (Yes/No)

⸻

Data & Cyber Security
	•	Do you have a cybersecurity framework? (Yes/No)
	•	Are you aligned to any standard (e.g. ISO 27001)?
	•	Do you use MFA for systems? (Yes/No)

⸻

Compliance
	•	Any regulatory breaches in last 3 years? (Yes/No)
	•	Any ongoing investigations? (Yes/No)

⸻

📦 SECTION 4: SUPPLY CHAIN & TRACEABILITY (YOUR EDGE)

Supply Chain Visibility
	•	Do you know the origin of all materials? (Yes/No)
	•	Do you audit your suppliers? (Yes/No)

⸻

Logistics
	•	Primary shipping method (Road / Sea / Air)
	•	Estimated annual freight emissions (if known)
	•	Do you track transport emissions? (Yes/No)

⸻

Risk
	•	Are any materials sourced from high-risk regions? (Yes/No)
	•	Conflict minerals involved? (Yes/No)

⸻

📊 SECTION 5: PERFORMANCE & IMPROVEMENT
	•	Do you have ESG targets? (Yes/No)
	•	Do you report ESG publicly? (Yes/No)
	•	What improvements have been made in last 12 months?

⸻

🧾 SECTION 6: EVIDENCE UPLOADS (MANDATORY FOR HIGH SCORE)

Require uploads for:
	•	Policies (H&S, Code of Conduct, etc.)
	•	Certifications
	•	Emissions reports
	•	Audit reports

⸻

🔐 SECTION 7: DECLARATION
	•	I confirm the information provided is accurate (Yes/No)
	•	Name of person completing
	•	Role
	•	Date

⸻

🧠 SCORING MODEL (IMPORTANT)

You don’t just ask — you score.

Example weighting:
	•	Environmental: 35%
	•	Social: 20%
	•	Governance: 25%
	•	Supply Chain: 20%

⸻

Scoring logic:
	•	Yes + evidence → full points
	•	Yes without evidence → partial
	•	No → zero
	•	Missing → penalty

⸻

Output:
	•	ESG Score (0–100)
	•	Risk Level:
	•	80+ → Low Risk
	•	50–79 → Medium
	•	<50 → High Risk

⸻

🔁 RECURRING LOGIC (12 MONTHS)

Each supplier must:
	•	Re-submit annually
	•	Update changes
	•	Upload new documents

Track:
	•	Last audit date
	•	Next due date
	•	Overdue status
